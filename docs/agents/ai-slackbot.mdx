## Add Tracecat to Slack

You'll need a saved [preset agent](/agents/ai-preset-agent#configure-a-preset), permission to edit agents in your Tracecat workspace (`agent:update`), and permission to install apps in the Slack workspace.

Free Slack workspaces can install up to 10 third-party or custom apps. If your workspace requires app approval, ask a Slack workspace owner to approve the install. See [Slack's workspace limits](https://slack.com/help/articles/115002422943-Usage-limits-for-free-workspaces) and [app approval settings](https://slack.com/help/articles/222386767-Manage-app-approval-for-your-workspace/).

1. Open `Agents` in the Tracecat workspace you want to connect. Select the `#` (Channels) view at the top of the page.
2. In the Slack card, choose a `Default agent` whose tools are appropriate for people who can message the app directly. Click `Add to Slack`.
3. In the same browser, check the Slack workspace shown on the authorization screen and click `Allow`. When you return to Tracecat, the card reads `Connected to Slack`.

![Slack installation card with a default agent selected and Add to Slack enabled](/img/agents/slack-add-to-workspace.png)

If the Channels view is missing, ask your platform administrator to check that your organization has the Agent Channels preview and that the [platform Slack app](/manage-platform/slack-app) is registered. You can also [connect a custom Slack app to one agent](/agents/channels#connect-a-slack-app).

## Get your first reply

1. Open a Slack channel whose members should be able to use the agent's tools and approve its calls. Run `/invite @Tracecat` and select the app.
2. Send `@Tracecat What can you help me with?` as a new channel message. The agent replies in a thread.
3. Mention `@Tracecat` again in that thread to continue the same session. You can also message the app directly to chat with the default agent.

To send new threads in a particular channel to a different agent, [set a channel route](/agents/channels#choose-which-agent-answers).
