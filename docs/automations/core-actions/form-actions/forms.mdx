## `core.forms.update_form`

Update or create a workflow's form and return it.

<Badge icon="lock" color="blue" size="lg" shape="pill">Enterprise Edition</Badge>

### Inputs

<ParamField path="workflow_id" type="string" required>

The workflow the form belongs to (short `wf_...` or full ID).

</ParamField>

<ParamField path="config" type="object | null">

The whole form config; it replaces the stored one. Keys, all optional: `title`, `description` (markdown), `success_message`, and `layout`, an ordered list of entries. An entry is one of `&#123;field: &lt;input name>&#125;`, `&#123;heading: &lt;text>&#125;`, `&#123;text: &lt;markdown>&#125;`, `&#123;notice: &lt;text>, tone: info|success|warning|danger&#125;` or `&#123;divider: true&#125;`. A field entry may add `label`, `help`, `placeholder`, `widget` (text, long_text, number, toggle, select, multi_select, cards, tags, date, datetime, user, file), `options` (`[&#123;value, label, description&#125;]`), `visible_if` (`&#123;field, equals&#125;`), `autofill` (requester, requester.user_id, requester.email, requester.first_name, requester.last_name, submission.id) or `hidden`. Workflow inputs left out of `layout` are appended in schema order. Omit `config` to leave it unchanged.

Default: `null`.

</ParamField>

<ParamField path="status" type="string | null">

`online` lets people submit the form (the workflow must be published); `offline` stops submissions. Omit to leave unchanged.

Default: `null`.

</ParamField>

### Examples

**Put a form online with a card picker**

```yaml
- ref: update_form
  action: core.forms.update_form
  args:
    workflow_id: wf_3mYp8JkL2nQr7sTu9vWx0Z
    status: online
    config:
      title: Laptop request
      layout:
        - field: model
          widget: cards
```
