## Overview

A Tracecat account authenticates a person, and an organization membership decides what that account can open. Registration and SSO create accounts. Invitations and superadmin provisioning create memberships.

## Registration

Self-service registration needs `basic` in `TRACECAT__AUTH_TYPES`. Without it the sign-up page shows `Sign up unavailable`. OIDC and SAML create the account on the first successful sign-in.

`TRACECAT__AUTH_ALLOWED_DOMAINS` is a comma-separated list of email domains permitted to create an account, and a blank value permits every domain. Tracecat applies the same list on every OIDC sign-in. SAML sign-in checks the organization's configured domains first. When none are configured, a single-tenant deployment falls back to this list and a multi-tenant deployment rejects the sign-in. An existing basic-auth account signs in with its password regardless of the list.

The first superadmin bypasses the domain list. A blocked email fails with the message `Please enter a valid email address.`

A password must be at least `TRACECAT__AUTH_MIN_PASSWORD_LENGTH` characters long, default `12`.

## Joining an organization

Registration and SSO create the account without an organization membership. Until an invitation or a superadmin grants one, the user sees `No organization access yet` after sign-in. That screen lists any pending invitations for their email and a `Sign out` button.

![No organization access screen](/img/manage-platform/users-no-org-access.png)

In a single-tenant deployment, a superadmin account joins the default organization as `organization-owner` at registration. With `TRACECAT__EE_MULTI_TENANT=true`, no account joins an organization automatically at registration. In both modes, registering through an invitation link joins that invitation's organization.

## Invite a member

Inviting requires `org:member:invite`, which `organization-owner` and `organization-admin` hold.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite dialog">
    Open `Members` in the organization sidebar (`/organization/members`) and click `Invite member`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the email and role grants">
    Enter the invitee's `Email`. Under `Role grants`, pick a scope and a role for each row. The scope is `Organization-wide` or one workspace. Click `Add grant` for another scope.

    <Frame>
      ![Invite member dialog with an email, a group, and two role grants](/img/manage-platform/users-invitations.png)
    </Frame>

    An invitation needs at least one role grant and takes one grant per scope. A workspace scope offers workspace roles only. `Groups` appears with [custom roles and groups](/manage-platform/custom-roles#invite-a-member-into-groups).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click `Send invitation`. The member appears in the table with the status `Invited`. The invitation stays valid for 7 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Tracecat rejects an email that is already a member or already holds a pending invitation. You can grant only roles whose scopes you hold yourself.

## Share or revoke an invitation

Tracecat emails the invitation when SMTP is configured. Open the invited member's row menu for these actions.

<Frame>
  ![Invited member row menu with copy link, resend email, and revoke actions](/img/manage-platform/users-invitation-actions.png)
</Frame>

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `Copy invitation link` | Copies `<app URL>/invitations/accept?token=<token>`. Without SMTP, send this link to the invitee yourself. |
| `Resend invitation email` | Sends the email again. Tracecat allows one resend per minute. |
| `Revoke invitation` | Cancels the invitation. The link shows `Invitation revoked`. |

## Accept an invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    The invitee opens the invitation link and signs in, or signs up with basic auth. A sign-up started from the link accepts the invitation during registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept">
    The signed-in email must match the invitation. A used, revoked, or expired link shows `Invitation already accepted`, `Invitation revoked`, or `Invitation expired`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After acceptance the account joins the organization, receives each role at its selected scope, joins the invitation's groups, and lands on `/workspaces`. A role the user already holds at a scope stays unchanged.

A platform admin console invitation with the `Organization member` role grants the baseline scopes and no role.

## Workspace membership

| Task | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Give a member a workspace role | Organization Members page: `Manage roles` in the member's row menu. See [Change a member's roles](/manage-platform/rbac#change-a-members-roles). |
| Add a member from a workspace | Workspace Members page: `Add member`. An existing organization member gets `workspace-editor` on that workspace. |
| Remove a member from a workspace | Workspace Members page: `Remove from workspace` in the member's row menu. |

`Add member` needs `workspace:member:invite` or `org:member:invite`. With `org:member:invite`, an email outside the organization gets an organization invitation with `workspace-editor` on that workspace.
