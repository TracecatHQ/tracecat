## Overview

A scope is one permission on one resource, named `resource:action`. A role bundles scopes. You assign a role to a user or a group, organization-wide or on one workspace.

Open `Members` in the organization sidebar (`/organization/members`) to manage members and their roles. The sidebar entry needs `org:member:read`.

## Built-in roles

Every organization has five preset roles. Tracecat rejects scope changes to a preset role and rejects deleting it.

| Role | Level | What it can do | Scopes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `workspace-viewer` | Workspace | Read workflows, integrations, cases, tables, schedules, agents, secrets, tags, variables, the inbox, the workspace, and its member list. | 12 |
| `workspace-editor` | Workspace | Everything `workspace-viewer` can, plus create and update those resources, run workflows, agents, and any action, terminate workflow runs, and run workflow sync and workspace sync. | 36 |
| `workspace-admin` | Workspace | Everything `workspace-editor` can, plus delete those resources, update or delete the workspace, remove members, and manage workspace service accounts. | 53 |
| `organization-admin` | Organization | Everything `workspace-admin` can in every workspace, plus create workspaces, manage organization settings, members and invitations, roles, groups, and assignments, SCIM, organization service accounts, the custom registry, and organization secrets, and read billing. | 82 |
| `organization-owner` | Organization | Everything `organization-admin` can, plus `org:delete`, `org:billing:update`, and `org:owner:assign`. | 85 |

The Scopes column counts the current definition. Startup seeding adds scopes a preset role is missing and removes none, so an organization created before a scope was renamed also keeps the old name.

Every member holds six baseline scopes without any role: `org:read`, `org:member:read`, `org:registry:read`, `org:secret:read`, `agent:read`, and `agent:execute`. The baseline grants no workspace access.

`organization-owner` and `organization-admin` reach every workspace in the organization. Every other member needs a workspace role, assigned on that workspace or organization-wide.

## Review members

The Members tab lists members and pending invitations.

<Frame>
  ![Members tab with Organization, Roles, Workspace, and Groups columns](/img/manage-platform/rbac-members.png)
</Frame>

| Column | Shows | Requires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Organization` | `Owner`, `Admin`, or `Member`, from the member's organization-wide role. | |
| `Roles` | Every role the member holds, directly or through a group. | `org:rbac:read` |
| `Workspace` | Every workspace the member can open. | `org:rbac:read` |
| `Groups` | The member's groups. | `org:rbac:read` on Enterprise |
| `Status` | `Active`, `Inactive`, or `Invited`. | |

Badges are abbreviated: role names to their initials, workspace and group names to their first three characters. Hover a badge for the full name. A cell shows three badges, then `+N` for the rest.

An invited row shows the roles, workspaces, and groups the invitation grants.

`Filter by email...` narrows the table by email. The `Role`, `Workspace`, and `Group` filters each take several values and match members that hold any of them.

## Change a member's roles

Changing roles requires `org:rbac:read` to open the dialog, `org:rbac:create` to add a role, `org:rbac:update` to replace one, and `org:rbac:delete` to remove one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage roles">
    On the Members tab, open the member's row menu and choose `Manage roles`.

    <Frame>
      ![Manage roles dialog with a direct role and two roles from a group](/img/manage-platform/rbac-manage-roles.png)
    </Frame>

    `Direct roles` lists roles assigned to the user. `Roles from groups` lists roles the user holds through a group, with the group name. Change those on the group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or replace a role">
    Under `Add role assignment`, select a scope of `Organization` or one workspace, select a role, then click the plus button. A user holds one direct role per scope, so a new role replaces the existing one at that scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a role">
    Click the minus button on a direct role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click `Done`. Tracecat saves all staged changes together. When the changes remove a role, confirm with `Confirm changes`. `Cancel` discards the staged changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A member with no roles stays in the organization with the baseline scopes. Use `Remove from organization` in the row menu to remove them.

## Assignment rules

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Role level | A role that holds any `org:*` scope is an organization role and is assigned organization-wide only. Every other role is a workspace role: assign it on one workspace, or organization-wide to grant it in every workspace. |
| One role per scope | A user holds one direct role per workspace and one organization-wide. The same limit applies to each group. |
| Effective scopes | A user's scopes are the union of their direct roles, the roles of every group they belong to, and the baseline scopes. |
| Granting | You can assign a role only when you hold every scope in it. Tracecat refuses the rest with `Cannot grant scopes not held by the caller`. Platform superusers are exempt. |
| Membership | You can assign roles and groups only to organization members. Tracecat refuses other users with `User not found in organization`. |

Tracecat rejects a role edit that would switch a role between organization and workspace level while it is assigned or offered in a pending invitation.

Without Enterprise, you manage access with the preset roles. See [Custom roles and groups](/manage-platform/custom-roles) for custom roles, groups, and scopes.

## Organization membership

Registration and sign-in without an invitation create no membership. A signed-in user without a membership sees `No organization access yet`, with any pending invitations listed. On a single-tenant deployment, registration or sign-in enrolls a superuser into the default organization.

Accepting an invitation creates the membership and applies its role grants at their selected scopes. Existing assignments at those scopes stay unchanged. A platform admin console invitation with the `Organization member` role creates the membership with the baseline scopes and no role. See [User management](/manage-platform/users#invite-a-member) for invitations.
