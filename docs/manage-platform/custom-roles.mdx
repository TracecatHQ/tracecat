## Overview

A custom role bundles the scopes you pick. A group holds members, and every member inherits the group's roles.

Open `Members` in the organization sidebar (`/organization/members`). The Roles and Groups tabs sit beside Members.

## Turn it on

Custom roles, groups, and scopes require the `rbac_addons` entitlement. A single-tenant deployment turns it on with `TRACECAT__FEATURE_FLAGS=rbac`, and a multi-tenant deployment reads it from the organization's tier.

Without the entitlement, or without `org:rbac:read`, the Members page shows the member table with no tabs.

## Create a custom role

Creating a role requires `org:rbac:create`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Roles tab">
    Open the Roles tab and click `Create role`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the role and set permissions">
    Enter a `Role name` and an optional `Description`. Set a level for each category under `Permissions`, or expand a category to select individual scopes.

    <Frame>
      ![Create role dialog with read access to workflows and write access to cases](/img/manage-platform/rbac-create-role.png)
    </Frame>

    | Category | Levels |
    | --- | --- |
    | Workflows, Agents | None, Read, Write, Execute, Admin, Custom |
    | Cases, Data, Secrets, Integrations, Workspace | None, Read, Write, Admin, Custom |
    | Organization | None, Read, Admin, Custom |
    | Actions | None, All namespaces, one namespace, Custom |

    You can grant only scopes you hold yourself. Tracecat refuses the rest with `Cannot grant scopes not held by the caller`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the role">
    Click `Create role`. The role appears in the list with its scope count and description. Expand a row to see its scopes.

    <Frame>
      ![Roles tab with a custom role expanded to show its scopes](/img/manage-platform/rbac-roles.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

A role with any `Organization` scope is an organization role and is assigned organization-wide only.

A custom role's row menu has `Copy role ID`, `Edit role`, and `Delete role`. Preset roles carry a `Preset` badge and have `Copy role ID` only.

Tracecat refuses deleting a role that is in use:

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| `Cannot delete role that is assigned to groups. Remove all group assignments first.` | Remove the role from each group. |
| `Cannot delete role that is assigned to users. Remove all user assignments first.` | Remove the role from each member. |
| `Cannot delete role offered in pending invitations. Revoke those invitations first.` | Revoke the invitations. |

## Create a group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the group">
    Open the Groups tab and click `Create group`. Enter a `Group name` and an optional description, then click `Create group`. This requires `org:rbac:create`. Tracecat refuses a duplicate name with `A group with this name already exists.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand the group">
    Click the group's row. It expands to a `Members` section and a `Roles` section.

    <Frame>
      ![Groups tab with a group expanded to show its members and roles](/img/manage-platform/rbac-groups.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Click `Add members`. Search by email or name, tick the members to add, then click `Add N members`. The header checkbox selects every listed member.

    <Frame>
      ![Add members dialog with two members selected](/img/manage-platform/rbac-group-add-members.png)
    </Frame>

    The dialog lists organization members who are not in the group. Pending invitations are not listed. When some members fail, the dialog stays open with those members selected.

    Adding and removing members requires `org:rbac:update`. You must hold every scope in the group's roles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign roles">
    In the `Roles` section, select a scope of `Organization` or one workspace, select a role, then click the plus button. This requires `org:rbac:create`.

    A group holds one role per scope. Tracecat refuses a second with `Group already has an assignment for this workspace`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click the minus button on a member or a role to remove it. Removing a role requires `org:rbac:delete`.

The group's row menu has `Copy group ID`, `Edit group`, and `Delete group`. Deleting a group removes its role assignments.

An identity provider manages the membership of a group mapped through [SCIM](/authentication/scim). That group shows `Membership is managed by your identity provider. Edit members there.` and has no `Add members` or remove buttons. You can still change its roles.

## Invite a member into groups

The invite dialog shows `Groups` when you hold `org:rbac:update` and `org:rbac:read`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite dialog">
    On the Members tab, click `Invite member` and enter the `Email`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select groups">
    Open `Groups` and tick each group the invitee joins.

    <Frame>
      ![Invite member dialog with a group selected above the role grants](/img/manage-platform/users-invitations.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add role grants and send">
    Add at least one row under `Role grants`, then click `Send invitation`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On acceptance the invitee receives the role grants and joins the groups.

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Offered groups | `Groups` lists the organization's groups, except groups an identity provider manages. |
| Scope ceiling | You must hold every scope in the selected groups' roles. Tracecat refuses the rest with `Cannot grant scopes not held by the caller`. |
| Changes before acceptance | Tracecat skips a group that was deleted or became identity provider-managed, and adds the invitee to the rest. |

## Custom scopes

Open `/organization/settings/rbac` and select the Scopes tab. It lists every scope in the organization, and `Filter by source` narrows it to Platform or Custom scopes. The Group assignments and User assignments tabs on the same page list every assignment.

Click `Create scope` to add your own. Names follow `resource:action` and use only lowercase letters, numbers, colons, underscores, dots, asterisks, and hyphens. Names cannot use the reserved prefixes `org:` or `workspace:`, including through a wildcard such as `*:` or `o*:`.

You can delete a custom scope. Platform scopes have no Delete action.

## In a workspace

A workspace's Members page lists users who hold a workspace role there: assigned on that workspace or organization-wide, directly or through a group. Organization owners and admins reach every workspace without appearing in this list.
